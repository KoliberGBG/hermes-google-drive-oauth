Privacy Policy

Hermes Google Drive is a private application used only by the account owner.

The application connects the owner's Google Drive account with a self-hosted Hermes Agent for personal file analysis and organization.

Google Drive data is not sold, shared, or made available to third parties.

Access credentials and OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's self-hosted server.

The application is not offered to the public and is intended solely for personal use by the account owner.
